## 2. Proof Gates Before Commercialisation

Commercial SaaS onboarding remains strictly disabled (`FEATURE_SAAS_ONBOARDING = false`) until the owner reviews evidence for all 6 gates:

- **Gate A — Traffic Integrity:** Real analytics ingestion, verified bot filtering, transparent UTM attribution.
- **Gate B — Repeatability:** Acquisition demonstrated across multiple sequential windows and campaigns.
- **Gate C — Cross-Brand Proof:** Verified workflows executed across CHATR, TalentXcel, and Nuty Tales without cross-brand leakage.
- **Gate D — Business Relevance:** Measured product activations, career actions, and purchasing customers (not just visits).
- **Gate E — Operational Safety:** 100% pass on error recovery, audit logs, credential safety, and kill-switch tests.
- **Gate F — Commercial Readiness:** Multi-tenant database isolation, delegated customer OAuth, and billing integration.
